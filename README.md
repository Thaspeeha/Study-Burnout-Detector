# 💬 Sample User Input

### 📝 Input 1

> **"I feel exhausted and unmotivated."**

### 📝 Input 2

> **"Pressured to do a research paper in 10 days."**

---

# 🚨 Output 1 — Burnout Warning Signs

## 💙 Here's What I'm Noticing

First, I just want to say — what you're feeling makes complete sense. Carrying a big deadline while already running on empty is genuinely hard, and it takes self-awareness to even pause and check in with yourself like this. That matters.

---

### 🔋 Your energy feels depleted before the work even begins.

Feeling exhausted going into a demanding task — rather than after it — can sometimes be a sign that your mind and body are already running low on reserves.

---

### 😔 Motivation seems to have quietly slipped away.

When the drive to do things you normally would do starts to fade, that's often your inner self waving a little flag, asking for rest and care.

---

### ⏳ The pressure feels time-compressed and intense.

A **10-day deadline** for a research paper is genuinely a lot.

When big demands stack on top of an already tired mind, it can make everything feel heavier than it normally would.

---

### ⚠️ The overall pattern

The combination of:

- 🔋 Exhaustion
- 😔 Low motivation
- ⏳ High pressure

is a pattern worth paying attention to.

These three together are some of the earliest whispers of burnout.

---

## 💛 A Gentle Note

> You don't have to push through this completely alone.

Please consider reaching out to:

- 🤝 A counselor
- ❤️ A trusted friend
- 👨‍👩‍👧 A family member
- 🎓 Your school's student support services

You deserve a soft place to land right now—not just a finish line to cross.

🌿 **You are so much more than your productivity.**

---

# 🌱 Output 2 — Recovery Plan

## 💙 Your Recovery Plan

First, I want you to know — feeling exhausted and unmotivated doesn't mean you're failing.

It means you're human, and you've been pushing hard.

Here's a gentle plan to help you move forward without burning out completely.

---

### 📄 1. Break the paper into tiny, non-scary pieces

Don't think:

> ❌ "10-day research paper."

Think:

- ✅ Today I will write one paragraph.
- ✅ Today I will find three sources.

Small daily targets feel manageable and build momentum.

---

### 🛌 2. Protect at least one hour of real rest daily

Not scrolling.

Choose something that genuinely recharges you:

- 🚶 Walk
- 😴 Nap
- 🎵 Music

This isn't wasted time—it's what makes the work possible.

---

### ⏱️ 3. Start with just 25 minutes of focused work

Try the **Pomodoro Method**:

🕐 25 minutes work

☕ 5 minutes break

When you're depleted, starting is the hardest part.

---

### 🚫 4. Say no to one unnecessary obligation this week

Just one.

Guard your energy like it's precious—because right now, it genuinely is.

---

### 💬 5. Talk to someone you trust

Even a five-minute conversation with a friend can lift the weight of isolation that exhaustion creates.

---

### 🎉 6. Celebrate small wins out loud

Every achievement matters.

Examples:

- ✅ Finished an outline
- ✅ Wrote 200 words

Your brain needs proof that you're making progress.

---

### ❤️ 7. Be deliberately kind to yourself

Remember to:

- 🍲 Eat something warm
- 🌙 Sleep before midnight when possible
- 💙 Remind yourself that done and imperfect beats perfect and never finished

---

# 🌿 Final Encouragement

> Recovery isn't a straight line, and you don't have to feel better all at once.

Every small, gentle step you take today is already enough.
